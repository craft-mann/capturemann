# CaptureMann vs Descript

*Checked Nov 11, 2026 · CaptureMann 0.3.2 · [all comparisons](README.md)*

**In short:** Descript is a cloud-based video and audio editor where you edit by editing the transcript, with strong AI
tools: Studio Sound, filler-word and retake removal, AI voices, Eye Contact and the Underlord co-editor. It runs on
macOS, Windows and the web, on a subscription with monthly media-minute and AI-credit limits. CaptureMann is a free,
open-source recorder that keeps everything on your computer: screenshots, recording, a focused editor, audio and meeting
recording, and local AI transcripts, translation, speaker labels and reports, on macOS, Windows and Linux. For heavy AI
editing and podcasts, Descript; for private, unlimited recording and transcription, CaptureMann.

## At a glance

| | CaptureMann | Descript |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free, no account | Free plan; Hobbyist $16 per person / month billed annually ($24 monthly); Creator $24 ($35); Business $50 ($65); Enterprise by quote |
| Free plan limits | None | 60 media minutes per month, 720p export, 5 GB storage, 100 one-time AI credits |
| Paid plan limits | None | Monthly media hours and AI credits (Hobbyist 10 h and 400 credits; Creator 30 h and 800; Business 40 h and 1,500) |
| Licence | Open source, Apache-2.0 | Proprietary |
| Platforms | macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 11 x64, Linux (Ubuntu 24.04+, Debian 13) | macOS 12+, Windows 10+, web; no Linux |
| Where files and processing live | Your computer | Descript's cloud |

## Feature comparison

| Area | CaptureMann | Descript |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Editing style** | Timeline editor: trim, cut, split, speed, backgrounds, zoom regions with suggestions, restyled cursor, 9:16 and 1:1 reframe; a caption editor | **Edit by editing the transcript**, plus a multitrack timeline and the **Underlord** AI co-editor |
| **Recording** | Display, window or region up to native resolution at 30 or 60 fps; webcam bubble; microphone and system audio on separate tracks | Screen recording, and **Rooms** for remote recording with several participants |
| **Screenshots** | Region, window and display with annotation and Auto-redact | Not found |
| **Transcripts and captions** | Local Whisper transcripts and captions (SRT, VTT, TXT, burned in), with no minute limit | Transcripts and captions in the cloud, within your plan's media minutes |
| **Audio clean-up** | Microphone noise suppression; silence and filler suggestions | **Studio Sound**, filler-word and retake removal |
| **Voice and video AI** | Not offered | **Overdub** voice clone, AI speech and avatars, Eye Contact, green screen, automatic multicam, generated video |
| **Translation** | Local: English via Whisper, other languages via the local text model | Translation (cloud) |
| **Meetings** | Audio-only recording; speaker labels, minutes and reports | Not its focus |
| **Sharing** | Local folders, Google Drive, YouTube (private by default) | Publishing and sharing from Descript |
| **AI agents** | 81 MCP tools running locally, and an identical CLI; risky actions need your on-screen confirmation | **Descript MCP** for Claude and other assistants, connected to your Descript account; imports and edits use your media minutes and AI credits |
| **Privacy** | No telemetry; no network traffic unless you connect a destination or download a model | Media is uploaded to and processed in Descript's cloud |

## Where Descript is stronger

- **Transcript-based editing** with a full multitrack timeline: the reference for editing talking-head video and
  podcasts.
- **Generative and audio AI:** Studio Sound, voice cloning, Eye Contact, avatars and the Underlord co-editor.
- **Remote recording** with Rooms, and team workspaces on higher plans.

## Where CaptureMann is stronger

- **No limits and no subscription:** unlimited recording and transcription, native resolution, no watermark.
- **Private by design:** everything runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded unless you choose a destination.
- **Linux,** plus screenshots with Auto-redact and meeting minutes in the same app.
- **A local MCP server** that needs no account or credits.

## Which to choose

| If you… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Edit podcasts or talking-head videos and want to cut by editing text | Descript |
| Want AI voices, Studio Sound or Eye Contact | Descript |
| Record and transcribe a lot without minute limits or a subscription | CaptureMann |
| Handle confidential recordings that must stay on your computer | CaptureMann |
| Work on Linux, or want screenshots and meeting minutes in the same app | CaptureMann |
