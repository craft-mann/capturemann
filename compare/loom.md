# CaptureMann vs Loom

*Checked Nov 11, 2026 · CaptureMann 0.3.1 · [all comparisons](README.md)*

**In short:** Loom is built for teams that send video messages: record, get a link, collect comments and reactions.
CaptureMann is built for people who want the recording itself: full resolution, no time limits, files on their own
computer, local AI transcripts and meeting reports, and an MCP server for AI agents. If your team lives on shared links
and comments, Loom fits better; if you want quality, privacy and no subscription, CaptureMann does.

## At a glance

| | CaptureMann | Loom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free, no account | Starter free; Business $18 per user / month ($15 billed annually); Business + AI $24 per user / month; Enterprise by quote |
| Free plan limits | None: no video count, length or resolution limit | 25 videos per person, 5 minutes per recording, up to 720p |
| Licence | Open source, Apache-2.0 | Proprietary (Atlassian) |
| Platforms | macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 11 x64, Linux (Ubuntu 24.04+, Debian 13; x86_64 and aarch64) | macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, Chrome extension |
| Where recordings live | Your computer, in a folder you choose; optional copies to Google Drive or YouTube | Loom's cloud |
| Account | Not needed | Needed |

## Feature comparison

| Area | CaptureMann | Loom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recording** | Display, window or region, up to your display's native resolution at 30 or 60 fps; pause, resume and crash recovery | Screen and camera; up to 720p on Starter, up to 4K on Business and above |
| **Webcam** | Live webcam bubble you can move, resize and reshape; recorded as its own track, so it can be changed after recording (Linux: camera track, no live bubble) | Camera bubble |
| **Audio** | Microphone with noise suppression and system audio, each on its own track; an audio-only mode for meetings, lectures and voice notes | Microphone and system audio |
| **Screenshots** | Region, window and display, with an annotation editor (arrows, shapes, text, pen, highlight, blur) and Auto-redact of personal data | Not checked |
| **Editing** | Trim, cut, split, speed, backgrounds, zoom regions with suggestions, restyled cursor, 9:16 and 1:1 reframe | Trimming and editing in the browser (details not checked) |
| **Export** | MP4 with platform presets, GIF, WebM; audio as M4A or WAV | Download from the Loom library (details not checked) |
| **AI** | On your computer, free: Whisper transcripts and captions (SRT, VTT, TXT, burned in), translation, speaker labels, meeting minutes and reports | In the cloud: titles, summaries, chapters and filler-word removal on the Business + AI plan |
| **Sharing and teamwork** | Local folders, Google Drive and YouTube (private by default); no hosted links, comments or view analytics | Instant share links, timestamped comments, emoji reactions, viewer insights, workspace library |
| **AI agents and automation** | 74 MCP tools plus resources and prompts, and an identical `capturemann` CLI; recording, publishing and deleting need your on-screen confirmation | Not found |
| **Privacy** | No telemetry; no network traffic unless you connect a destination or download a model | Recordings are uploaded and stored by Loom |

## Where Loom is stronger

- **Sharing is the product.** A link the moment you stop, with comments, reactions and view counts. CaptureMann has no
  hosted links yet.
- **Teams and integrations.** Workspaces, admin controls and Atlassian integrations for companies already on Jira and
  Confluence.
- **Mobile and browser.** Apps for iOS and Android and a Chrome extension; CaptureMann is desktop only.

## Where CaptureMann is stronger

- **No limits on the free version:** any length, any number of recordings, up to native resolution, including 4K.
- **AI included and private:** transcripts, translation, speaker labels and meeting minutes run on your computer at no
  cost, not on a $24 plan in the cloud.
- **Meetings and voice notes:** an audio-only mode with reports, which Loom does not focus on.
- **Your files, your storage:** self-contained bundles in your own folder, copies to your own Google Drive.
- **Linux, and agents:** runs on Linux, and an AI agent can record, edit and publish through MCP.

## Which to choose

| If you… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Send quick video updates to a team and want comments and view counts | Loom |
| Record tutorials or lessons longer than 5 minutes, in high resolution, without paying | CaptureMann |
| Need transcripts or meeting notes that never leave your computer | CaptureMann |
| Work on Linux or want an AI agent to drive the recorder | CaptureMann |
| Record from a phone or a Chromebook | Loom |
