# How CaptureMann compares

CaptureMann is a free, open-source (Apache-2.0) app for screenshots, screen recording and audio recording on macOS,
Windows and Linux, with local AI transcripts, speaker labels and meeting reports, and every feature available to AI
agents through MCP and a CLI. These pages compare it with the five tools people most often ask about.

| Compare with | Best known for | One-line difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Loom](loom.md) | Async video messages with instant share links | Loom hosts and shares for teams; CaptureMann keeps files on your computer, with no length or quality limits |
| [Snagit](snagit.md) | Screenshots, annotation and step guides | Snagit has the deepest screenshot toolset; CaptureMann adds full recording, local AI and agents for free |
| [CleanShot X](cleanshot-x.md) | Polished Mac screenshots and recordings | CleanShot X is the Mac polish benchmark; CaptureMann is cross-platform with local AI |
| [Cap](cap.md) | Open-source Loom alternative | Both are open source; Cap focuses on hosted sharing, CaptureMann on local AI, audio and agents |
| [Screen Studio](screen-studio.md) | Beautiful product-demo videos | Screen Studio is the benchmark for motion polish; CaptureMann covers screenshots, audio and AI too |

## How these pages are written

- **CaptureMann facts** come from this repository at version 0.3.1 (the [README](../README.md), the
  [user guide](../docs/user/user-guide.md) and the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md)). Platform limits are stated as the README
  states them.
- **Competitor facts** come from the competitor's own website or repository where we could read it, otherwise from a
  dated third-party listing, named in each page's Sources section. Anything we could not confirm is marked
  "not checked".
- **Checked on Nov 11, 2026.** Prices and features change often; the competitor's own site is always the current
  source. Found something wrong or out of date? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/craft-mann/capturemann/issues).
- We try to be fair: every page has a section on where the other tool is stronger.
