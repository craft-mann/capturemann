# CaptureMann vs CleanShot X

*Checked Nov 11, 2026 · CaptureMann 0.3.1 · [all comparisons](README.md)*

**In short:** CleanShot X is the benchmark for fast, polished screenshots and short recordings on the Mac, with scrolling
capture, text recognition, a quick-access overlay and cloud links. It is Mac only. CaptureMann runs on macOS, Windows
and Linux, records up to native resolution with a webcam bubble, and adds local AI transcripts, meeting minutes and an
MCP server for AI agents, for free. If you live on a Mac and mostly take screenshots, CleanShot X is hard to beat; if you
record, work across systems or want local AI, CaptureMann covers more.

## At a glance

| | CaptureMann | CleanShot X |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free, no account | One-time licence (listed at $29 by third-party sites; check cleanshot.com for the current price) plus an optional Cloud subscription (listed at $8 / month or $96 / year) |
| Licence | Open source, Apache-2.0 | Proprietary |
| Platforms | macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 11 x64, Linux (Ubuntu 24.04+, Debian 13) | macOS only |
| Where files live | Your computer, in a folder you choose; optional copies to Google Drive or YouTube | Your computer; CleanShot Cloud for links |

## Feature comparison

| Area | CaptureMann | CleanShot X |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screenshots** | Region, window and display | Region, window, display, **scrolling capture** |
| **Annotation** | Arrows, rectangles, ellipses, text, pen, highlight, blur | Arrows, text, shapes, blur, background removal (full list not re-checked) |
| **Redaction** | **Auto-redact** proposes blurs for 20 kinds of personal data and secrets, on your computer; you accept each one | Manual blur |
| **Text from images** | **Copy text**: select a region or the whole screenshot and copy the text, recognized on your computer (macOS: Apple Vision; Windows: the OCR languages installed in Windows; Linux: `tesseract`, English by default); not compared on accuracy | Text recognition (OCR) |
| **After a capture** | Saved to your library; copy, annotate or export from there | Quick-access overlay in the corner to annotate, copy or share at once |
| **Screen recording** | Display, window or region up to native resolution at 30 or 60 fps; pause, resume and crash recovery; microphone with noise suppression and system audio on separate tracks | Screen recording with audio and a webcam overlay; MP4 or GIF; trimming |
| **Editing** | Trim, cut, split, speed, backgrounds, zoom regions with suggestions, restyled cursor, 9:16 and 1:1 reframe | Trimming (other recording effects not re-checked) |
| **Audio and meetings** | Audio-only recording; transcripts, speaker labels, minutes and reports | Not found |
| **AI** | Local and free: Whisper transcripts and captions, translation, speaker labels, meeting reports | Not found beyond text recognition |
| **Sharing** | Local folders, Google Drive, YouTube (private by default) | CleanShot Cloud links with password protection and custom domains |
| **AI agents and automation** | 74 MCP tools and an identical CLI; risky actions need your on-screen confirmation | Not found |

## Where CleanShot X is stronger

- **Mac polish and speed.** A refined capture flow with the quick-access overlay, built only for macOS.
- **Screenshot extras.** Scrolling capture, which CaptureMann does not have yet.
- **Instant cloud links** with passwords and custom domains.

## Where CaptureMann is stronger

- **Cross-platform:** the same app on macOS, Windows and Linux.
- **Recording depth:** native-resolution recording, a webcam bubble that stays editable after recording, auto-zoom,
  vertical reframing and platform export presets.
- **Local AI and meetings:** transcripts, translation, speaker labels and meeting minutes, all on your computer.
- **Free and open source, and agent-ready** through MCP and a CLI.

## Which to choose

| If you… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Use only a Mac and mostly take screenshots and short clips | CleanShot X |
| Need scrolling capture | CleanShot X |
| Work on Windows or Linux, or across systems | CaptureMann |
| Record tutorials or meetings and want transcripts or minutes | CaptureMann |
| Want an AI agent to drive captures and recordings | CaptureMann |
