# Transcription quality plan (0.2.7): parameters, audio preparation, normalisation, hallucinations, 0.2.6 vs 0.2.7

Status: **draft for the maintainer** (Nov 4, 2026). Written from the code on `main` (whisper-rs 0.16.0 over whisper.cpp 1.8.3, `crates/cm-ai`), the reports in `verification/benchmarks/` and the review brief. Every number below is "measured" (one machine, mostly one run per cell); nothing is "verified". Private meeting audio and text never enter the repository: counts and rates only.

Rules that bind every item: general techniques only (no phrase lists, no channel names, no per-language blocklists, no one-recording fixes); everything local; no GPL in shipped binaries; models are user downloads with pinned URL and SHA-256; each change gets a before/after report before it merges; every user-facing feature has an action, a CLI command and an MCP tool with the same behaviour.

## 1. Diagnosis: why Turbo-class models invent outro text

Whisper is trained on web audio where quiet tails are followed by outro captions; the decoder's language-model prior fills a window that gives it no acoustic evidence. Each mechanism below is a hypothesis with a test; "tested" marks what the reports already cover.

| # | Mechanism | App controls it? | Confirming test | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Prior over near-silence**: a 30 s window with little speech energy; the 4-layer Turbo decoder is less robust than the 32-layer Large v3 | Only indirectly (what audio reaches the window) | Count insertions over reference-silent regions per model on the same audio (needs the public repro of §5) | Not tested: no repro set; Large v3 had fewer insertions on the meeting (38 vs 56), weak evidence |
| H2 | **Zero padding**: the last window of every piece and every re-read is padded with 30 s of digital zeros (`whisper.cpp` line 3197); the model has seen padded tails followed by outros | Yes: piece boundaries, re-read spans, and whether a piece ends in speech | Check whether the three stored hallucination times (1:34, 4:19, 4:53 of the Nov 2 meeting) fall in the last 30 s of a piece or inside a re-read span; then decode the same spans with real room tone appended instead of zeros | Not tested (the stored transcript has piece boundaries only implicitly: 300 s cuts, so 4:19 and 4:53 are inside piece 1 and not a padded tail; 1:34 is not either. H2 alone does not explain them) |
| H3 | **Previous-text conditioning**: the window inherits up to 224 tokens of earlier text; once an outro appears it is re-fed | Yes (`n_max_text_ctx`) | Already measured: `n_max_text_ctx 0` reduced meeting deletions but *added* 10 words over silence on the noise set | Tested; not the main driver of P1 (it drives P2 loops) |
| H4 | **The glossary prompt** on every piece pushes the decoder toward "caption-like" output | Yes | Measured with a made-up prompt only: Turbo meeting 22.4 vs 22.7, English 16.0 vs 6.2 (the prompt hurts English). The real glossary was never measured | Half tested; the real glossary run is step W2 below |
| H5 | **Lift gain** raises noise floors to speech level so the window looks like speech | Yes (`ai.lift_quiet_input`) | Insertions rose 31 to 56 on the meeting with the lift on; compare insertions over silent regions with lift on/off once the silence metric exists | Partly: the rise is measured, its location (silence or speech) is not |
| H6 | **Temperature fallback** samples (t ≥ 0.2) on a window that fails the entropy/log-prob check and produces fluent nonsense; carry-over is dropped above t 0.5 | Yes (`temperature_inc`, thresholds) | Log the temperature at which each segment was accepted (not available from whisper-rs today; needs the segment callback or a fork); correlate with insertions | Not tested; "fallback off" made loops worse in one probe |
| H7 | **No-speech handling is AND-ed** with log-prob, so the `<\|nospeech\|>` signal is never acted on for confident text | Not through parameters; only by reading `no_speech` ourselves (`Transcript.segments`, already carried) | Distribution of `no_speech` on hallucinated vs quiet real segments on the real meetings | Partly: the gate using it cost real speech, but thresholds were not tuned per duration |
| H8 | **Timing collapse**: prior-driven text has no acoustic anchor; DTW stacks it | Yes (we read DTW times) | 18 words in 0.9 s is on record; measure rate for the outro units | Partly: the collapse-only gate was cheapest (noise 8.0 %, 1 word) and cost 0.5 point on FLEURS |

Conclusion: the app controls H2 to H8; H1 is the model. The two experiments that are missing and cheap are H2 (where the three instances sit relative to piece and re-read boundaries, and zeros vs room tone) and the insertion-over-silence location for H5. Everything else is blocked on a reproduction set (§5).

## 2. Pillar 1: parameter search (disciplined, not a grid)

Parameters available through whisper-rs 0.16 (`whisper_params.rs`): sampling strategy (`Greedy{best_of}`, `BeamSearch{beam_size, patience}`), `temperature`, `temperature_inc`, `entropy_thold`, `logprob_thold`, `no_speech_thold`, `n_max_text_ctx`, `max_initial_ts`, `length_penalty`, `audio_ctx`, `max_len`, `split_on_word`, `max_tokens`, `single_segment`, `suppress_blank`, `suppress_nst`, `token_timestamps`, `thold_pt`/`thold_ptsum`, `initial_prompt`, `language`/`detect_language`, `grammar`, VAD. Not available: `carry_initial_prompt`, `suppress_regex` (fork needed; `suppress_regex` would also be a phrase mechanism and is out by the open-source rule).

**Evaluation sets (fixed before any run):** T = tuning: meeting-5min (private, counts only), fleurs4min (4 clips), english syn4, noise (9). H = held-out, never used to pick: fleurs `viu_h`-style second set of 4 new FLEURS clips, AMI `IS1009b`, PriMock day3, the second private meeting (Nov 2, once its 5-min reference exists), and the public silence-insertion set of §5 once built. Each cell: WER (fold), **WER-exact** (new: diacritics and `đ` kept, case and punctuation folded), ins/del/sub, words over reference-silent spans, loop runs, coverage, calls, RTF, peak MB. Runs per cell: 1 on this machine (three repeats were identical in every earlier cell; repeat 3x only for a candidate that is about to be adopted, and on a second Mac if one exists).

**Hard acceptance gate for any parameter change:** on H, meeting WER-exact not worse by more than 0.5 point, no public set worse by more than 0.5 point, silence insertions not up, RTF not below 0.8x of the baseline. A change that wins on T and fails on H is reported and not merged.

**Order (coordinate descent, one axis at a time, baseline = shipped stack):**

| Step | Axis and values | Why this axis | Expected cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | `logprob_thold` ∈ {-1.0, -0.7, -0.5} × `no_speech_thold` ∈ {0.6, 0.4} (the AND means both must move together) | The only pair that changed anything so far (-2 points on quiet clips, removed one loop) | 6 cells × T |
| P2 | `temperature_inc` ∈ {0.2 (default), 0.1, 0 (no fallback)} with `entropy_thold` ∈ {2.4, 2.0} | H6; fallback sampling is the only source of randomness in greedy decode | 6 cells |
| P3 | Fallback-only beam: greedy first; on a window that fails P2's thresholds, re-decode with `BeamSearch{5, patience 1.0}`. whisper.cpp does this internally when `best_of`/`beam_size` > 1 (temperature 0 uses beam, > 0 uses sampling), so measure `BeamSearch{5}` outright against greedy and against `best_of 5` | Beam reduces repetition in the literature; cost 1.6 to 2x | 3 cells; RTF matters |
| P4 | `n_max_text_ctx` ∈ {16384, 64, 0} **per model** | 0 helped Turbo (21.0) and hurt Large v3 (25.4): the per-model default may differ; 64 is the untested middle | 6 cells (2 models) |
| P5 | `max_initial_ts` ∈ {1.0 (default), 0.5} and `length_penalty` ∈ {-1 (none), 0.6} | Cheap; `max_initial_ts` limits how late the first token may start, which caps text that "starts" in a silent tail | 4 cells |
| P6 | Prompt: none / real glossary / real glossary only on the first piece of a run (emulating `carry_initial_prompt=false` + context) | H4; the English harm is on record; the real glossary is untested | 3 cells, needs the maintainer's glossary export (counts only) |
| P7 | `audio_ctx` ∈ {1500 (default), 768} on the re-read path only (short spans) | Shorter audio context speeds short re-reads; known to cost accuracy on full windows, so never for the main path | 2 cells on re-read spans |
| P8 | `suppress_nst` off | M2i says "measured, not assumed": letting the model write its own `[MUSIC]`-style tokens may absorb non-speech windows that otherwise become outros; the tokens are dropped by `build_words` already | 1 cell on noise set + meeting |
| P9 | Language: `vi` fixed vs `vi,en` detected on the piece with the highest speech share (not the first piece) | P3 of the brief; the first-piece rule is a bug in waiting (English small talk at the start) | 2 cells, 1 h row |

Not in the search: `max_len`/`split_on_word` (formatting only; captions are rebuilt from words), `single_segment` (kills timestamps), flash attention (77 invented words), Silero as decode unit (worse), `suppress_regex`.

**Which model file is the Vietnamese default?** The data support a *per-language* hint, not a general switch: Large v3 wins on the one private Vietnamese far-field clip by 5.6 points and loses on English and on read Vietnamese. Decision procedure: (a) a second independent Vietnamese far-field reference (the maintainer; §8 Q1); (b) run Turbo, Large v3 and a **Q8_0 Turbo and Q5_0 Large v3** quantisation (both are standard whisper.cpp conversions; each needs a pinned URL and SHA-256 in `models.json` before it is offered) on T and H; (c) PhoWhisper medium/large only after its own decoding path exists (no timestamps, DTW heads unknown: `far-field` and `short-clips` show PhoWhisper-small at 31 to 63 % WER with our settings, which is the decode, not the model). Rule: the Vietnamese default moves to Large v3 if it beats Turbo by ≥ 3 WER-exact points on both private references and FLEURS-h is within 2 points; otherwise the already-shipped label ("Recommended for Vietnamese meetings. Slower.", commit 8a46f40) stays as the only change. Per-model parameter sets are justified only if P4 confirms the Turbo/Large split on H; then `PipelineSettings` gains a per-model table (defaults in code, overridable in `settings.json`, no new UI).

Run count: about 45 cells on T (≈ 45 × 7 clips × ~20 s = 2 h of compute with Turbo; Large v3 rows 5x) plus H for the two or three survivors. Two agent days including the reports.

## 3. Pillar 2: audio preparation (ranked)

Timing rule for every step: the pipeline edits a **copy** for decoding and keeps an `offset map` (piece start, trimmed spans) so `file_to_session` is applied to decoded times unchanged; nothing is ever cut from the stored files. A step that changes sample positions (trimming, splitting) must be a `Vec<(decoded_range, source_range)>` map applied in `transcribe_pieces`, tested with made-up tokens.

| Rank | Step | Status and evidence | Vietnamese risk | Accept / reject measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Raw mic vs denoised copy on a real recording** (`original_audio` exists) | Untested on real pairs: the imported meeting has identical files; the synthetic gate showed denoised worse by 1.3 to 3.1 points and 3 to 7 coverage points | Denoisers remove soft finals and unvoiced onsets (p/t/c/ch), which carry Vietnamese word identity; low | Needs a recorded `.cmrec` with both `mic.wav` and `mic.denoised.wav` (the maintainer records one meeting with consent, or the 20-min M2h check): WER-exact and coverage both ways; adopt raw as default if it wins by ≥ 1 point on two recordings and loses nowhere |
| 2 | **Lift per speech region instead of per call** (region = energy VAD region, gain capped +15 dB, 200 ms ramps) | Per-call lift shipped; it raised insertions 31 to 56 on the meeting while cutting deletions. Per-region gain lifts quiet speakers without lifting the room between them | Tones ride on F0, unaffected by gain; low | Silence insertions and deletions on T; accept if deletions stay down and silence insertions fall back toward the no-lift count |
| 3 | **High-pass 80 Hz** (first-order, exists as `Normalize.high_pass_hz`) | Mixed: English 16.0 to 7.1, short FLEURS 12.1 to 12.9, meeting no change | Removes rumble, not tones (F0 100 to 300 Hz); but a first-order filter at 80 Hz still shaves 1 to 2 dB at 150 Hz: try 60 Hz second-order | Keep only if short FLEURS is unchanged within 0.3 |
| 4 | **VAD as veto, not as cutter** (Silero or the speaker engine's segmentation marks speech; decode untouched 30 s windows; words whose span has < 10 % speech evidence from *two* sources are re-read with no prompt, then become `[noise]`) | The gate failed because one signal (Silero or energy) alone flagged 5 % of real units; the stored data show the **speaker engine** separates the known hallucinated unit (27 % speech) from quiet real speech (71 %) in the one pair we have | Quiet speakers are exactly what single-signal VAD misses; requiring agreement of two signals before veto protects them | Hypothesis; needs the silence set of §5. Accept if silence insertions fall ≥ 50 % with real-set WER-exact within 0.5 |
| 5 | **Trim long silences (> 8 s) to 2 s** before the window is formed, keeping the offset map | Removes the condition of H1/H2 (windows that are mostly room tone) without cutting speech | Low if the trim keeps 1 s of padding on both sides | Insertions over silence and WER-exact; cheap to build on `vad::speech_regions` |
| 6 | **Split pieces at pauses so no window ends in silence longer than 5 s** (extend `pieces::plan` with a "end the piece just after speech" rule) | H2; the current cut is "quietest 100 ms near 300 s", which maximises the padded-silence tail | None | Count of pieces ending inside > 5 s silence (before/after) and silence insertions |
| 7 | **Loudness normalisation** (`normalize_loudness` exists) | Mixed on synthetic quiet clips (-4.8 to +3.0) | None | Only as part of rank 2 |
| 8 | **Dereverberation (WPE)** | No permissive Rust implementation; Python prototype only | Could smear tones; medium | Offline prototype on the private clips; port only if ≥ 2 points |
| 9 | **Noise suppression before Whisper** (RNNoise BSD-3 is what the recorder already uses; DeepFilterNet is MIT/Apache; both permissive) | The denoised copy is already a suppression; rank 1 decides whether suppression helps at all | High (see rank 1) | Rank 1 first |
| 10 | Channel handling: decode mic and system as **two streams**, merge words by time | Not measured; `mix_at` creates artificial overlap in hybrid meetings | None | WER-exact on a recording with both tracks (the maintainer's EmeSoft meeting has system audio); 2x decode cost on overlapping speech |
| 11 | Resampling quality and dithering | FFmpeg `-ar 16000` uses swresample's default (good); dither is irrelevant for f32 | None | Skip |

Why the earlier negatives are not the end: Silero-as-cutter failed because `min_silence 150 to 200 ms` fragments Vietnamese syllable-timed speech into 72 calls and clips onsets; the gate failed because every signal was used alone and on segments of any length. Variants that can still work: rank 4 (two-signal veto on units ≥ 3 s only), rank 5 (trim only > 8 s), rank 2 (gain per speaker region). Per-speaker normalisation (gain by diarization segment) is a later variant of rank 2 and needs the speaker pass before decoding, which the daemon runs in parallel today; defer.

## 4. Pillar 3: AI normalisation of the transcript (word-preserving)

Goal: punctuation, capitalisation, sentence and paragraph breaks and obvious fused/split-word repairs, in Vietnamese and English, from the local text model, **without adding, removing or replacing content words**.

- **Input unit:** one speaker turn (or 30 s when no speakers), joined with its neighbours' last sentence as read-only context, batched to ~40 % of the model context like `chunk.rs`. The model receives plain lowercase-stripped-of-nothing text (words as decoded) and returns the same words with punctuation, case and `\n` paragraph breaks, as a JSON schema `{ "text": string }` at temperature 0 (the existing `http.rs` path).
- **Invariant (checkable, enforced):** fold both sides with `measure::fold` minus the diacritic folding (i.e. keep tone marks, drop case and punctuation), then word-level edit distance between input and output must be **0 substitutions and 0 deletions**; insertions are allowed only for punctuation tokens that fold to nothing. A turn that fails is kept raw (`normalized: false` on that turn) and counted in the job result. This is the same pattern as `report/check.rs` (names and citations must exist in the transcript).
- **Alignment back to words:** because the invariant forbids content changes, the output tokens map 1:1 onto `Word`s in order; each word gains `display: Option<String>` (the cased/punctuated form) and the turn gains `breaks: Vec<usize>` (paragraph breaks after word index). Times, confidence and speaker never change. A tone-mark change is a substitution under the invariant and is rejected (so the model cannot "fix" tones, which would guess).
- **Storage:** `transcript.json` stays the source. A sidecar `transcript.normalized.json` `{ version, model, language, turns: [{ start, end, speaker?, display: [..], breaks: [..], normalized: bool }] }` is written by the job; `get_transcript` returns `normalized?: true` and `get_captions` gains `source: "normalized"` ranking between generated and edited (edited always wins). Captions built from `display` where present; reports, translation and search keep reading `words` (search also indexes `display` for case-insensitive hits, no change in behaviour). Undo: writing the sidecar is one revision tagged `user` or `agent:<name>`, like `set_captions`. A re-transcribe deletes the sidecar.
- **Action surface:** `normalize_transcript { capture_id, language?, request_id? }` → `{ job_id }` → job result `{ turns, normalized, rejected, model, seconds }`; Free tier (mutating, so the user can set it to Ask); CLI `capturemann ai normalize <id> [--wait]`; MCP tool of the same name; `transcribe` and `import_media.after` gain `normalize?: bool`. Setting `ai.normalize_after_transcribe` (default **off** in 0.2.7: the measurement does not exist yet and the text model is a 1.6 GB optional download; on by default is a 0.2.8 decision from the report).
- **UI:** one switch on the AI page ("Tidy punctuation and casing with the text model") and a "Tidy" button in the transcript panel that runs the action; a turn that was rejected shows raw. The desktop owner wires it (outside `mac-ai`).
- **Failure modes and guards:** small-model hallucination (caught by the invariant); translation drift (Gemma may answer in English for Vietnamese input: the invariant catches it as substitutions); dropped sentences (deletions, caught); Vietnamese tone-mark damage (substitution, caught); reply truncated (`output_too_long` halving already exists in `translate.rs`; reuse); a turn over 400 words is split at the longest pause first.
- **Speed budget (from the measured 57 to 100 tok/s):** the output is as long as the input, so cost ≈ words × 1.3 tokens / 60 tok/s. 15 s (40 words): < 2 s. 15 min (2,000 words): ~45 s. 1 h (8,000 words): ~3 to 4 min, after a 1 h transcript that itself takes ~3 min on Turbo. Acceptable as an explicit action; too slow to be silent by default on 1 h until measured.
- **Evaluation:** (a) WER-exact invariance = 0 by construction, report the rejected-turn rate (target < 5 %); (b) punctuation F1 (`.`, `,`, `?`, sentence boundaries) and casing accuracy against a reference with punctuation: FLEURS vi and LibriSpeech have cased, punctuated transcripts, so both languages have public references; the private 5-min reference supplies the meeting row (counts only); (c) a 50-turn human spot-check by the maintainer scored "better / same / worse" per turn. Store as `verification/benchmarks/normalisation-<date>.md`.
- **Which model is enough:** Gemma 4 E2B is the only pinned model (decision O2). The test that tells whether it suffices is (b) and the rejected rate: if F1 < 0.7 on Vietnamese or rejections > 10 %, the report says so and a larger local model becomes a 0.2.8 pin request (no cloud).

## 5. Hallucination handling as its own track

**Signals, ranked by the evidence we have (all general, none lexical):**

1. **Two-source speech evidence disagreement**: words whose span has < 10 % speech by both the speaker engine's segmentation (pyannote, already linked) and Silero, on units ≥ 3 s. The one stored pair separates (27 % vs 71 % speaker-engine share); hypothesis until the §5 set exists.
2. **Perturbation agreement (ensemble of two decodes)**: re-decode the suspect unit at -6 dB and with a 150 ms offset, no prompt; audio-driven text comes back with ≤ 20 % word edit distance, prior-driven text changes or vanishes. Cost: two short decodes per suspect unit (units are ~5 % of the transcript, so ~10 % extra compute).
3. **Second-model verifier**: Large v3 on suspect units only (it has fewer insertions on the meeting); same cost logic.
4. **Runtime silence signature**: already built in the gate; keep as a third vote, never alone.
5. **Timing collapse / words per second**: cheapest, already in `failure_kinds`; keep.
6. `no_speech` and `avg_logprob` per segment: carried but weak alone (the gate result).
7. Level vs speaker norm: tie-breaker only (loops sit at -29 to -37 dBFS).

Design: a unit becomes a sound (`[noise]`/`[unclear]`) only when **two independent votes** agree (1 and 2, or 1 and 3); one vote only triggers the re-read. This is the gate re-built as a voting rule on long units, which is the untried variant.

**Public reproduction (the blocker for every number in this section):**
- (a) **Real room tone, consented, no speech:** the maintainer records 10 × 60 s of empty meeting room / laptop fan / keyboard / chairs / distant corridor with the laptop mic (no voices), publishes them on `huggingface.co/craftmann` under CC0 with pinned SHA-256; the repo keeps a `tests/tools/make_silence_set.py` that mixes FLEURS vi and LibriSpeech utterances at -25 to -40 dBFS into 60 to 120 s clips with 20 to 60 s room-tone gaps, with a `speech.json` marking reference-silent spans. The metric is **insertions per hour of reference-silent audio** (words and sentence units). Real room tone, not `anoisesrc`, is what the earlier set lacked.
- (b) **Synthetic far field:** OpenSLR SLR26/28 impulse responses (Apache-2.0) convolved with the same utterances, MUSAN (CC BY 4.0) babble at SNR 5 to 15 dB, DEMAND (CC BY-SA 4.0, benchmark use only) room recordings; 0/20/40 % overlap. Gives the far-field WER row that FLEURS cannot.
- (c) **A consented hand-made set:** two or three colleagues, one laptop at 3 m, 10 minutes of scripted and free Vietnamese and English conversation with long pauses, transcribed by hand by the speakers; published only if every speaker signs off, otherwise local like VIVOS.

**Decision rule for Turbo (per language, by data):** Turbo stays the default for a language only if, on ≥ 2 real meetings in that language with the shipped stack, invented-text units over reference-silent regions are **≤ 2 per hour** and WER-exact is within 3 points of the best offered model. Today: Vietnamese fails the WER half (5.6 points behind Large v3 on one clip; a second reference is needed to confirm) and the insertion half is unmeasured (no reference-silent regions marked on the private meetings yet: the maintainer marks silent spans in the 5-min reference, §8 Q2). English passes the WER half (Turbo best on 7 of 9) and the insertion half is unmeasured. So: **demote Turbo for Vietnamese only when the second reference confirms, by making Large v3 the suggested model for `vi` in the picker**; do not remove Turbo (it is the English default and 5x faster). Meanwhile (already shipped or cheap): the "Recommended for Vietnamese meetings" label on Large v3; a one-line hint under the model list that quiet far-field audio can produce text nobody said and that `[noise]` marks replace it when the pipeline is sure; the failure counters in the job result so a user can see "n units re-read, m marked as sound".

## 6. The 0.2.6 vs 0.2.7 comparison (so the maintainer can see it)

**Builds.** `git worktree add ../capturemann-wt/v0.2.6 v0.2.6`; in it `make sherpa` and `cargo build --release -p cm-daemon -p cm-cli`; `main` likewise. Two headless daemons, never the app: `CAPTUREMANN_CONFIG_DIR=$S/cfg-026 CAPTUREMANN_LIBRARY=$S/lib-026 CAPTUREMANN_SOCKET=$S/026.sock cm-daemon` and the same for `027`; both config dirs get a `settings.json` with the same `ai.languages` (`vi` for the Vietnamese rows, `en` for English), the same active model, the same glossary, and the models folder pointed at one shared read-only copy (if 0.2.6 cannot share the folder, copy the two model files; nothing is downloaded). Each library is a copy of the test bundles (`cp -R`, private bundles stay on the maintainer's disk under `$S`). Per bundle: `capturemann ai transcribe <id> --model large-v3-turbo --wait`, `ai speakers --wait`, `ai transcript` → JSON into `$S/out-026/<clip>.json` and `out-027/`. 0.2.7 rows repeated with `--model large` (Large v3). A script `tests/tools/compare_releases.py` (new, reads both JSON shapes: 0.2.6 has no `sounds`/`segments`) computes the metrics and writes the table; `loop_probe` and `failure_kinds` supply loops and counters.

**Audio set.** Vietnamese: the two private meetings (EmeSoft Nov 2, 45 min; Meeting_with_PAT_TLSG, scored on its 5-min reference plus counts over the full file); FLEURS 4-min clips as the read-speech control. English: AMI `ES2004b`, `TS3003a`, `IS1009b` (far-field array mix, CC BY 4.0, word references), PriMock57 day1 and day3 (CC BY 4.0), syn4 (synthetic 5-min meeting). Short rows: one 15 s FLEURS and one 20 s English clip. Good additions: one **real English far-field meeting recorded by the maintainer with consent** (a weekly call captured with the laptop mic plus system audio, 10 minutes, participants transcribe their own turns), because AMI is headset-array English and PriMock57 is near-field; and LibriSpeech `test-other` concatenations for noisy read English.

**Metrics per clip and release:** WER (fold) and **WER-exact** with ins/del/sub; invented-text insertions over reference-silent spans (private meetings: once the maintainer marks silent spans in the 5-min reference; else "n/a"); loop runs and dense repeats; coverage %; sounds written (0.2.7 only); speaker count found vs known and DER where turn references exist (AMI, PriMock57); RTF; peak RSS (`/usr/bin/time -l`); for the normalisation (if built): punctuation F1, casing accuracy, rejected-turn rate. Runs: 1 per cell, 3 for the two private meetings (loops are the one place runs may differ).

**Report format:** `verification/benchmarks/release-compare-0.2.6-vs-0.2.7-<date>.md` following `verification/benchmarks/README.md`: §1 short answer (one sentence per language), §2 setup with the exact commands, §3 data and licences, §4 one table (rows = clips, columns = 0.2.6 Turbo / 0.2.7 Turbo / 0.2.7 Large v3 × metrics), a per-meeting counts block for the private rows (words, insertions, deletions, loops, sounds, speakers found, seconds), and for **public clips only** up to two short diff excerpts per clip (reference / 0.2.6 / 0.2.7 at one time), §5 what it does not show, §6 decision. The maintainer's "see it" view is that table plus a `diff -u` of the two public-clip TXT exports kept under `$S` (never committed).

**What I would not do:** remove Turbo (English default, 5x faster, best on 7 of 9 English rows); ship the single-signal gate or Silero-as-cutter again; tune anything on the 5-min reference alone (it is seeded from a machine transcript and 1,000 words: 1 point = 10 words); let the text model touch content words or tone marks (the invariant forbids it); run a 3 h soak or more FLEURS-only rows (read speech is solved to ~10 %); fork whisper-rs for `suppress_regex`; add any lexical rule anywhere.

**Top risks:** (1) the private references are biased toward the old Large v3 output (its 12.6 % vs 17.1 % re-run), so Turbo-vs-Large gaps may be overstated by a few points: W10's independent reference is the fix; (2) the room-tone set may still not provoke the outro text (H1 may need real speech-then-silence transitions from the same room): then (c) of §5 is required and the Turbo decision waits; (3) normalisation on a 1 h transcript costs ~4 min of text-model time, and Gemma may reject many Vietnamese turns: default off protects users, the report decides; (4) the comparison depends on 0.2.6 reading the same models folder: a copy costs 5 GB of disk; (5) all numbers are one M4 Pro; an Intel row for RTF only is worth adding if a machine is available.

## 8. Questions I could not answer from the repository

1. **Is there a second Vietnamese far-field meeting with a reference not seeded from a machine transcript?** It decides the Vietnamese default (§2, W11) and halves the bias risk.
2. **Can the maintainer mark the reference-silent spans (start, end, seconds only) in the 5-min reference and around the three outro instances of the Nov 2 meeting?** Without them the insertion-over-silence metric has no real-audio row and the Turbo decision rule cannot be applied.
3. **Which glossary terms and `ai.languages` value were active for the Nov 2 run and the older Large v3 transcript?** Decides whether the prompt (H4) is a contributor and explains the 12.6 vs 17.1 % gap.
4. **Does a recording with both `mic.wav` and `mic.denoised.wav` exist (or can one be made with consent)?** Rank 1 of §3 cannot be measured on imported audio; it decides the default track.
5. **Is a 1.6 GB text-model download and ~4 min per hour of audio acceptable for a default-on tidy step, or is it an opt-in button?** Decides W9's default and whether W8 needs a streaming progress event.
6. **May the room-tone recordings and a consented 10-minute conversation be published on `craftmann` (CC0 / CC BY)?** Without a public set, every hallucination number stays private and the open-source rule ("no fixes for one recording") cannot be shown to hold.
